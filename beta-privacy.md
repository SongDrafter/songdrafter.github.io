# SongDrafter Beta – Privacy Notice

*Last updated: [DATE]*

This notice explains what happens to your data when you apply for the SongDrafter beta and take part in it. It covers the beta application form, the TestFlight beta and the questionnaire at the end. The app itself is covered by the separate [SongDrafter Privacy Policy](/).

## Who is responsible for your data

SongDrafter is made by one person, who is the controller of your data:

**BARNABAS PRIFER**
2120 DUNAKESZI, SZANTO U 15, Hungary
Email: songdrafter.app@gmail.com

For any question about your data, or to use any of your rights below, write to songdrafter.app@gmail.com.

## What data we collect

**From the application form:**
- your first name and email address;
- your Instagram or TikTok handle, if you give it;
- your iPhone model, iOS version and the headphones you use;
- your answers about how you make music and capture ideas;
- whether you would like to share a video made with SongDrafter;
- the name you'd like to be credited with in the app, if you give one.

**During the beta, through Apple TestFlight:**
Apple shares some information with us about testers, such as your email address (if you were invited by email), your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight. Apple's own processing is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**From the end-of-beta questionnaire:**
your answers about how SongDrafter worked for you.

**If you message us:** the content of your emails or direct messages.

We don't ask for your full name, address, phone number or payment details, and we don't collect any data about you from other sources.

## Why we use it

- to choose testers so that the beta covers a range of iPhones, iOS versions and headphones;
- to send you the TestFlight invite and talk to you about the beta;
- to find and fix bugs, using crash reports and your feedback;
- to send you the end-of-beta questionnaire;
- to give you the full version of SongDrafter free, as promised to founding testers;
- to credit you in the app, only if you asked to be credited and only with the name you gave.

Testers are chosen by hand. No automated decision-making or profiling is used.

We will never sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Legal basis

We process your data based on your **consent** (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give when you submit the form. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to songdrafter.app@gmail.com. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before, but it means you leave the beta.

## Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run the beta. They only process your data on our behalf:

- **Google Forms** – stores the form answers;
- **Google Gmail** – for emails;
- **Apple (TestFlight)** – distributes the beta app and collects crash reports and feedback;
- **Instagram / TikTok** – if we talk through direct messages there.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. In those cases the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Form answers, questionnaire answers and messages:** until the beta ends and you've received the full version, and at most **[3] months** after the beta ends. Then we delete them.
- **Credit name:** for as long as it's shown in the app, or until you ask us to remove it.
- **TestFlight data:** Apple keeps beta builds and their data for up to 90 days per build.

If you leave the beta earlier, we delete your data within 30 days, except what we need to keep for a legal reason.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:
- get a copy of your data (access);
- have incorrect data corrected;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict how we use it;
- receive it in a portable format;
- object to how we use it;
- withdraw your consent at any time.

Write to songdrafter.app@gmail.com and we'll answer within one month.

If you think we've handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority:

**Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH)**
1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, Hungary
[naih.hu](https://naih.hu)

You can also complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live.

## Age

You must be at least **16** to apply for the beta.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date at the top and tell beta testers about important changes by email.
