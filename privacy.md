# SongDrafter Privacy Policy

_Effective 22 November 2026_

SongDrafter is a multitrack idea recorder for iPhone. It is built to work
entirely on your device.

## What SongDrafter does not do

- It has **no account** and no sign-in.
- It does **not collect** any personal data, usage data, analytics or
  crash data of its own, and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It does **not send** your recordings, drafts or any other data to the
  developer or to any third party. There is no server.

## Microphone

SongDrafter uses the iPhone's built-in microphone only while you record a
take, use the tuner, or measure your headphones' timing. The audio is
processed and stored on your device. To line takes up with Bluetooth
headphones, the app analyses the recording locally; the result (a latency
value for each pair of headphones, with its name and Bluetooth identifier)
is stored on your device only.

## Photos

When you save a video of a draft, SongDrafter asks for permission to **add**
it to your photo library. It never reads your existing photos.

## Data stored on your device

Your drafts, recordings, folders, settings and headphone timing values are
stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are included in your
device backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's control. Deleting the app
deletes them. Drafts you delete stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then
are removed.

## Sharing you start

Export, Share as Video and other share actions hand a file to the iOS share
sheet; where it goes (Mail, Messages, Files, another app) is your choice and
is governed by that service's own policy.

In beta (TestFlight) builds, the Headphone timing screen offers **Send
Timing Report**: it creates a text file describing how your takes were
lined up (app and iOS version, device model, the current audio output and
its reported latencies, stored headphone values with headphone names and
Bluetooth identifiers, track names with their timing values, and the app's
timing log). It contains no audio. Nothing is sent unless you choose a
destination in the share sheet. If you send it to the developer, it is used
only to fix timing problems and is deleted when no longer needed.

TestFlight itself may share crash reports and feedback you submit with the
developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

## In-app purchases

If SongDrafter offers a purchase, it is handled by Apple. The developer does
not receive your payment details.

## Children

SongDrafter does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
